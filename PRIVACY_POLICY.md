# WeightKit – Privacy Policy

_Effective date: 6 October 2026 · Last updated: 16 October 2026_

This privacy policy explains how the **WeightKit – Metal Weight Calculator** app for Android and iPhone ("WeightKit", "the app") handles information. The app is published by **GGSHOP LIMITED** ("we", "us", "our"), which is the data controller for the data described in this policy.

Contact: **ggshoplimitedco@gmail.com**

## 1. Summary

- Your calculations, lists, prices, quotes (client, VAT, notes), company details, logo and custom materials stay **on your device**. There is no account and we never receive this content.
- The **free version shows ads** from **Google AdMob**. AdMob collects device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads (see section 5).
- The **Android app** sends a **hashed device ID** to our own server to run the free trial and to verify Google Play purchases (see section 4).
- Purchases are processed by **Google Play** or the **Apple App Store**. We never see your payment details.
- We do **not sell** your personal information.

## 2. Information stored only on your device

Everything you enter in the app – dimensions, materials and densities, prices and currency, cut lists, waste, markup and VAT values, quote details (client name, quote number, validity, notes), company name, address and contact details, the logo image you choose, custom materials, theme and unit settings – is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We do not receive it.

- **PDF and CSV export:** files are created on your device. They leave your device only if you choose to share them (for example by e-mail or a messaging app) through your phone's share sheet; the app you share with handles them under its own privacy policy.
- **Logo:** if you add a logo for your PDF quotes, you pick the image yourself in the system photo picker. The app receives only that one image (it has no access to the rest of your photos), stores a reduced copy in its private folder and uses it only in PDFs you create.
- **Backup file (Pro):** "Back up all lists" creates a file on your device with your lists, company details, logo and custom materials. It leaves your device only if you share or save it somewhere yourself. "Restore lists from file" reads only the file you pick.
- **Phone backups:** your phone's own backup (Android Auto Backup to your Google account, or iCloud / device backup on iPhone) may include the app's settings and lists. These backups are handled by Google or Apple under their privacy policies; we cannot access them.
- **iPhone free trial:** on iPhone you start the 7-day trial yourself with a free in-app item ("7-day Trial") from the App Store. Its start date comes from Apple's signed purchase record and a copy is kept in the device Keychain. This stays on your device and with Apple; nothing is sent to us.
- Deleting the app deletes this data from the device (except a Keychain entry on iPhone, which iOS may keep, and any phone backups you have made).

## 3. Permissions the app uses

- **Internet / network state** – to load ads, to check purchases with the store and (Android) to reach our trial and purchase server.
- **Advertising ID (Android, `AD_ID`)** – used by Google AdMob in the free version (section 5).
- **No tracking on iPhone** – the iPhone app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission and does not give the advertising identifier (IDFA) to ads, so ads on iPhone are not personalised with it.

The app does **not** use location, camera, microphone, contacts or any account login. It has no access to your photo library or your files: it only receives an image or a backup file that you pick yourself (see section 2).

## 4. Free trial and purchase verification (Android only)

To prevent the free trial from restarting after "Clear data" or reinstalling, and to confirm Google Play purchases, the Android app connects to our server (**apps-api**, operated by GGSHOP LIMITED) over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

**What is sent:**

- a **device hash** – a one-way SHA-256 hash of your device's Android ID combined with the app name. The Android ID itself never leaves your phone and cannot be recovered from the hash;
- the platform (Android) and the build type;
- only when you buy Pro: the **product ID** and the **Google Play purchase token**.

**What our server keeps:** the device hash, the trial start date, and – if you buy Pro – the product ID, the purchase token, the purchase status and the devices (as hashes) that use the purchase (up to 6 devices per purchase), with the time each was last used. Like any internet service, the server also receives your **IP address** when the app connects; it is used only to deliver the response and to protect the service against abuse (for example rate limiting), and it is not used to identify you or for advertising.

**Why:** to provide the free trial fairly, to restore your Pro purchase on a new or reset device without an account, and to prevent fraud.

**Shared with:** Google Play only – the purchase token is sent to Google Play's purchase-verification service to check that the purchase is genuine, not refunded and not cancelled. We do not share this data with anyone else and we do not use it for advertising.

**How long:** while you use the app and for as long as needed to keep your trial and purchase status working (including restoring Pro on a new device), and as required for legal, tax or accounting purposes. You can ask us to delete it at any time (section 9). If you delete it, the trial protection and automatic purchase restore for that device stop working; a valid purchase can be restored again through Google Play.

The iPhone app does not use this server.

## 5. Advertising (free version only)

The free version of WeightKit shows a small number of ads served by **Google AdMob** (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). Users with **Pro**, an active **free trial** or an active **24-hour reward** see no ads.

**Ad types:** a small banner on the Lists screen; occasionally a full-screen ad when opening the Lists screen (at most once every 48 hours); and an optional rewarded ad that you can choose to watch to unlock Pro for 24 hours. No ads are shown on the calculator or during a calculation.

**Data AdMob may collect:** your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID; on iPhone the IDFA is not used), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version), and ad interaction data (ads shown, clicks, viewing time). Google uses this data to show, measure and improve ads, to limit how often you see an ad, and to detect fraud and abuse. Google may use cookies or similar technologies (such as the advertising identifier) for this purpose.

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. Learn more:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google advertising: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

**Your choices:**

- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** before any ad is requested, you are asked for consent through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform). You can choose non-personalised ads or refuse personalised advertising. You can change your choice at any time in the app under **Settings → Ad privacy settings**.
- **iPhone:** the app does not track you and does not use the IDFA.
- **Android:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (**Settings → Google → Ads**, or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**, depending on the device).
- **Remove ads completely:** buy WeightKit Pro.

## 6. Purchases

WeightKit Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by **Google Play** (Android) or the **Apple App Store** (iPhone). We do not receive your name, e-mail address or payment details. The app asks the store whether Pro has been purchased; on Android, the purchase token is also verified through our server as described in section 4. Refunds are handled by Google or Apple under their policies; a refunded purchase removes Pro.

- Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

- **Performance of a contract / providing the app you requested** – the free trial, Pro unlock, purchase verification and purchase restore (sections 4 and 6).
- **Legitimate interests** – preventing fraud and abuse of the free trial and purchases, keeping the service secure, and showing non-personalised ads that fund the free version.
- **Consent** – personalised advertising and the use of the advertising identifier where consent is required (section 5). You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings, without affecting earlier processing.
- **Legal obligations** – keeping purchase-related records where the law requires it.

## 8. Sharing, sale and international transfers

We share data only with: **Google** (AdMob advertising and Google Play purchase verification) and **Apple** (App Store purchases), as described above, and with our hosting provider, which runs our server on our behalf. We may disclose information if required by law.

We **do not sell** personal information and we do not use your data for our own advertising profiles. Under some US state laws (for example California), the use of advertising identifiers by AdMob for personalised ads may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can opt out by turning off ad personalisation or deleting/resetting your advertising ID in your device settings (section 5) or by buying Pro.

Google, Apple and our hosting provider may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the UK equivalent.

## 10. Security

Data sent between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). The device ID is hashed on your device before it is sent. Our server stores no names, e-mail addresses, payment details, location or advertising identifiers, and access to it is restricted. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the data.

## 11. Children

WeightKit is a professional tool for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 12. No automated decisions

We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you based solely on automated processing.
